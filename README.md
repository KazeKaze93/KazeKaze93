## 🎴 Style Grid

A visual card grid in place of the style dropdown: search, categories, favorites, presets, wildcards and
preview thumbnails for your prompt styles. One idea, two hosts.

## 💬 Comic Lettering

Image models draw speech bubbles full of gibberish. Comic Lettering finds those bubbles, wipes them clean and
fits your text inside. It also adds captions and sound effects, and lets you place bubbles of your own in a
visual editor.
